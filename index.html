How the Schedule Exposure Score is calculated
Score = Float Exposure + Downstream Exposure + Longest Path Factor + Status Factor (out of 100). It is an indicative planning aid, not a statistical probability of delay.
Float Exposure (0 to 40): 40 at zero or negative float, easing down to 0 at 30 days of float.
Downstream Exposure (0 to 30): scaled by successor count divided by the largest successor network in the file.
Longest Path Factor: adds 20 when P6 flags the activity on the driving path.
Status Factor: adds 10 when in progress, 5 when not started, 0 when complete.
Priority: High 70 and above, Medium 40 to 69, Low below 40.

How it works

A quick guide to each part of the tool.

Loading a file

Drop a Primavera P6 XER export onto the page. It is read on your own device and nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Summary and health

The top row shows the project, its data date, activity counts and the current critical count. The health line warns when large negative float points to a hard finish constraint or a stale data date.

Find an activity

Search by ID or name to open a full detail card for any activity: its float, dates, downstream size, risk score and a plain reason. Clicking a bar in the timeline opens the same card.

Today's schedule brief

A short written summary of what is critical, what is near critical, and the activity carrying the most exposure.

Top 10 schedule risks

Activities ranked by a Schedule Exposure Score built from float, downstream size, longest path and status. Open the methodology for the formula, and press Why on any row for a plain reason.

Critical and near critical

Lists critical and near critical activities. Choose the definition, total float or longest path, and the window: day, month or year.

Programme timeline

A Gantt of the activities coloured by criticality, with the data date marked. Click a bar to open that activity.

Delay simulator

Pick an activity and a delay to see how many downstream activities would lose their remaining slack. It is an indicative estimate, not a P6 reschedule.

Export and print

Save the brief or the top 10 as CSV, or use Print to save the whole view as a PDF.
